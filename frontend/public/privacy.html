Wisdom & Word

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026 · Effective immediately

This policy explains what information Wisdom & Word ("we", "us", "the app") collects when you use our website and mobile app, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep things minimal on purpose.

Short version: You can use Wisdom & Word without creating an account. We store an anonymous browser ID so you can pick up where you left off, and we send your questions to a Bible-focused AI so it can respond. If you subscribe from the Amazon Appstore edition, Amazon processes the payment via In-App Purchasing — we never see your card. If you ask us to read counsel aloud, ElevenLabs converts the text to speech. That's it.

1. Who we are

Wisdom & Word is operated by the stewards of the wisdominword.com service. For any privacy question, write to privacy@wisdominword.com.

2. What we collect & why

2.1 Anonymous session identifier

When you open Wisdom & Word, we generate a random anonymous ID (for example, anon-a3f4-9b12) and store it in your browser's local storage under the key wisdom_user_id. This lets you continue a session, receive counsel history, and — if you subscribe — keep your Pro status.

  • What it is: a random string. It is not tied to your name, email, phone, IP, or device fingerprint.
  • Where it lives: your browser only, plus our database when linked to a subscription.
  • How to clear it: clear your browser's site data for wisdominword.com. This will start a fresh anonymous session.

2.2 Questions and counsel

When you ask a question, the text of that question is sent to our servers and forwarded to a large-language-model provider (currently Anthropic Claude, accessed via Emergent) so it can generate a Bible-grounded response. Both your question and the counsel returned are stored in our database, associated with your anonymous session ID, so you can revisit past reflections.

  • We do not use your questions to train AI models.
  • Anthropic's own retention policy applies to data in transit through their API. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

2.3 Voice playback (optional)

When you tap "Listen" on a counsel, the answer text is sent to ElevenLabs which converts it to speech and returns an MP3 stream. We do not store the audio. See ElevenLabs' Privacy Policy.

2.4 Payments (optional, only if you subscribe)

In this Amazon Appstore edition of Wisdom & Word, subscriptions are processed by Amazon In-App Purchasing. Payment is charged to your Amazon account. The subscription state is managed for us by RevenueCat, which receives from Amazon only a receipt identifier, product SKU, and an anonymous app-user ID we generated in your device. We never see or store your Amazon credentials, card number, CVC, or billing address. See Amazon's Privacy Notice and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

2.5 Email

This Amazon Appstore edition does not ask for your email address. Amazon sends receipts to your Amazon account directly. We do not send marketing emails.

2.6 Shared verse pages

If you click "Get Link" on a verse, we create a public shareable landing page with that verse and — if you allowed it — the image you generated. That page is intentionally public (anyone with the link can view it). We keep a simple view counter but do not identify who viewed it.

2.7 Basic analytics

We use PostHog to understand aggregate usage — for example, how many people ask a first question, complete a subscription, or hit an error. PostHog respects your Do Not Track preference where applicable. See PostHog's Privacy Policy.

2.8 Technical logs

Our servers keep short-lived request logs (IP, timestamp, path) for security and abuse-prevention. These logs are automatically rotated and are not tied to your anonymous session ID.

3. What we do not collect

  • Your name, phone number, home address, or government ID
  • Your location
  • Access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files
  • Advertising identifiers or third-party ad-tracking data
  • Health, biometric, or financial information beyond the Amazon In-App Purchasing flow

The Wisdom & Word Android and iOS apps request no runtime permissions beyond standard network access.

4. Who we share with

We share only what is necessary to run the service, with the processors named above:

  • Anthropic (via Emergent) — receives your question text to generate counsel
  • ElevenLabs — receives counsel text to generate voice, only if you tap Listen
  • Amazon In-App Purchasing — processes your payment via your Amazon account, only if you subscribe
  • RevenueCat — receives your Amazon receipt to verify and track your subscription state, only if you subscribe
  • MongoDB Atlas / hosting provider — stores your session data
  • PostHog — receives anonymous product analytics

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers.

5. Data retention

  • Conversation history: retained while your anonymous session ID exists. Clearing your browser storage effectively orphans the data.
  • Subscription records: retained for tax, accounting, and refund purposes as required by law (typically 7 years).
  • Shared verse pages: retained indefinitely unless you request deletion.
  • Server logs: rotated within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (EU/UK GDPR, California CCPA, Virginia, etc.) you have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data. To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@wisdominword.com from the address associated with your subscription (or include your anonymous session ID if you have not subscribed). We respond within 30 days.

You may also cancel your subscription at any time from Your Amazon → Memberships & Subscriptions — this stops future billing immediately and ends premium access at the end of the paid period.

7. Children

Wisdom & Word is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). If you believe a child has provided us data, contact privacy@wisdominword.com and we will delete it.

8. Security

All communication with our servers is encrypted in transit via HTTPS. Payments are handled entirely by Amazon In-App Purchasing using Amazon's PCI-compliant infrastructure. No system is perfectly secure — but we do not store card data, do not collect sensitive personal information, and keep the data we do hold to the minimum required.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States, the European Union, or other jurisdictions. By using Wisdom & Word you consent to your data being transferred, stored, and processed in those regions.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Wisdom & Word after a change indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
